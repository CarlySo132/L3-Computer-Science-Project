Badminton Rules

The winner in a badminton match is whichever team gets to 21 points first, and they must win by a 2 point lead. Exemplar, if the score is 21-13 then the player with 21 won. However if the score were to be 20-20, then someone gets 21, the match isn't over yet it is only once one of the two players reaches a two point lead. An example being 21-23 or 30-32, the match will continue as long as it takes until there is a two point lead, in which, the player has then won.

Badminton Court and Serving rules

Singles court

Tramlines are out — use the inner sidelines.

Doubles court

Full width is in — use the outer sidelines.

Singles serve

Serve diagonally into the long narrow box.

Doubles serve

Short & wide — the back tramline is out.

Umpire Hand Signals

In

Arm points sharply down toward the line where the shuttle landed.

Out

Both arms extended horizontally out to the sides.
