Badminton Rules

Here are the rules for Badminton:

  • Points are first to 21
  • Must win by 2 points
  • When your score is odd you must serve from the left side
  • When your score is even you must serve from the right side
    • Singles court

    Tramlines are out — use the inner sidelines.

    Doubles court

    Full width is in — use the outer sidelines.

    Singles serve

    Serve diagonally into the long narrow box.

    Doubles serve

    Short & wide — the back tramline is out.
